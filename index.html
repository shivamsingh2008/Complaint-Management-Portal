Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding how the campus grievance portal works.

How long does it take to resolve a registered complaint?
Urgent issues (such as electricity, water, or medical emergencies) are addressed within 2 to 6 hours. Standard maintenance and academic queries are resolved within 18 to 24 working hours.
Can I lodge an anonymous complaint on ResolveHub?
While your Registration Number is required to verify that you are a bonafide student, your identity is kept strictly confidential from third parties and is only visible to the Chief Grievance Officer.
What happens if my complaint is not resolved on time?
Our portal features a 48-hour Auto-Escalation matrix. If a ticket remains unresolved past its SLA deadline, it is automatically escalated to the Head of Department (HOD) and Dean Student Welfare.
